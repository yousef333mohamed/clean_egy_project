# Workforce forecast baseline

Purpose: recent rolling-average staffing requirements by region/shift. It cannot assign workers and must never support disciplinary decisions. No approved training period or outcome-backed MAE/RMSE/interval coverage exists. Forecast-date information is excluded. Sparse attendance, workload, and shift data are known risks; subgroup performance is unknown. Human supervisory review and rollback are mandatory. Feature version 1.0.0; model version unregistered; approval unapproved.
