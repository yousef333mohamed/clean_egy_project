# Model evaluation

Evaluate only on chronological holdouts. Classification reports ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, precision, recall, F1, Brier score, and false-negative rate. Workforce reports MAE, RMSE, and interval coverage when outcomes exist. Anomaly accuracy is not claimed until reviewed incident labels exist.

Report region, waste type, capacity group, truck, shift, horizon, and sensor-status slices where sample sizes permit. Weak slices remain visible. A candidate must improve the selected business metrics over its baseline, maintain acceptable calibration, pass leakage checks, and avoid critical subgroup failure. Training score alone is never a promotion reason.
