# Synthetic Demo: Missed-Collection Incident Procedure

> This document is synthetic test data. It is not an official company policy and is not legally or operationally authoritative.

## Purpose

Demonstrate a documented response to a reported missed waste collection while preserving a clear operational audit trail.

## Scope

This sample applies to training incidents involving a scheduled bin or service area. It does not replace approved complaint, emergency, or public-safety procedures.

## Procedure

1. Record the service date, region, bin or location identifier, complaint reference, and reporting channel.
2. Verify the planned route and available trip log without changing the original operational record.
3. Check whether access restrictions, vehicle failure, unsafe conditions, or an emergency caused the missed service.
4. Assign a recovery action with an owner and target completion time.
5. Update the training incident after service is completed, including the completion timestamp and supporting reference.
6. Close the incident only after the recovery action has been confirmed.

## Escalation rules

- Escalate immediately when the report involves medical waste, fire, hazardous material, or blocked emergency access.
- Escalate repeated misses at the same location to the operations supervisor.
- Notify complaint resolution when the promised recovery time cannot be met.
