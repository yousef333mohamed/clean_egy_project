# Synthetic Demo: Truck Pre-Trip Inspection

> This document is synthetic test data. It is not an official company policy and is not legally or operationally authoritative.

## Purpose

Demonstrate how a maintenance knowledge document can describe a consistent pre-trip inspection for a collection vehicle.

## Scope

This demo covers training vehicle `TRK-DEMO-01` before departure. Qualified personnel must use the approved real-world manual instead of this sample.

## Procedure

1. Verify that the vehicle identifier, assigned route, and inspection date match the dispatch record.
2. Walk around the vehicle and check tires, lights, mirrors, visible leaks, body damage, and safety markings.
3. Confirm engine oil, coolant, hydraulic fluid, fuel, and warning indicators according to the manufacturer documentation.
4. Test service brakes, parking brake, steering, horn, reversing alarm, camera, and communication equipment.
5. Confirm that the first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, reflective equipment, and spill kit are present for this demo.
6. Record each result as pass, fail, or not applicable; do not conceal a failed item.

## Escalation rules

- Do not dispatch the demo truck when a safety-critical item fails.
- Refer fluid leaks, brake warnings, steering faults, or damaged tires to a qualified maintenance supervisor.
- Notify dispatch if inspection findings will delay the planned route.
